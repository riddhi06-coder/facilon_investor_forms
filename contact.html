Contact principles

Structured and Purpose-Specific

Collect only what is needed

General contact should not become a document-upload or onboarding channel.

Keep provider onboarding separate

Detailed KYC, product and onboarding documentation should be collected through the appropriate provider journey.

Route privacy requests correctly

Privacy and data-rights requests should enter the designated privacy workflow rather than ordinary sales or support handling.

Preserve investor control

A general enquiry should not automatically create a provider introduction or disclose investor information to a Service Provider.
