Cookie Policy

Facilon Cookie Policy

Last Updated: January, 2026

1. Introduction

Facilon Services Private Limited (“Facilon”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary to ensure the secure, reliable, and lawful operation of its websites, applications, and online portals (collectively, the “Platform”).

Currently, Facilon uses only strictly necessary cookies.

We do not use functional, analytics, advertising, or marketing cookies.

This Cookie Policy explains what strictly necessary cookies are, why we use them, and how they support the delivery of our services.

2. What Are Strictly Necessary Cookies?

Strictly necessary cookies are cookies that are essential for the basic functioning of the Platform. Without these cookies, the Platform cannot operate securely or provide the services requested by users.

These cookies are always active and do not require user consent, as they are required to deliver core services and meet security and regulatory obligations.

3. How We Use Strictly Necessary Cookies

Strictly necessary cookies are used solely for the following purposes:
  • Enabling secure user authentication and login
  • Maintaining user sessions and session integrity
  • Protecting the Platform against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Supporting load balancing and infrastructure stability
  • Preventing cross-site request forgery and similar security threats
  • Remembering privacy and consent preferences
  • Ensuring compliance with applicable legal, regulatory, and audit requirements
These cookies:
  • Do not track users across websites
  • Do not profile users
  • Are not used for advertising or marketing
  • Are not used for analytics or behavioural measurement

4. Legal Basis for Use

The use of strictly necessary cookies is based on the following legal grounds, as applicable:

  • India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023):

Legitimate use / necessary for the provision of requested services

  • European Union & United Kingdom (GDPR and ePrivacy laws):

Necessary for the performance of a contract and/or Facilon’s legitimate interests in ensuring platform security and functionality

  • Other jurisdictions:

Necessary for service delivery, security and operational integrity

5. Third-Party Essential Cookies

Certain strictly necessary cookies may be set by trusted third-party service providers (such as cloud infrastructure, security, or identity and access management providers) solely to enable authentication, security controls, and platform availability.

Such third parties process data only on Facilon’s instructions and in accordance with applicable data protection laws and contractual safeguards.

6. Managing Cookies

Because strictly necessary cookies are essential for the operation of the Platform, they cannot be disabled through cookie preference settings.

Users may configure their browser settings to block or delete cookies; however, doing so may result in parts of the Platform not functioning correctly, including secure login, session continuity, and access to user accounts.

7. Future Use of Non-Essential Cookies

If Facilon introduces any functional, analytics, or marketing cookies in the future:

  • This Cookie Policy will be updated accordingly
  • Users will be provided with clear information and appropriate consent controls
  • Non-essential cookies will be deployed only after valid user consent, where required by law

8. Updates to This Cookie Policy

Facilon may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or platform functionality.

The “Last Updated” date at the top of this policy will indicate when changes take effect.
