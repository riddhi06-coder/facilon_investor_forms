Who Facilon can support

One Facilitation Layer Across Multiple Provider Types

Different investment routes require different regulated and operational participants. Facilon is designed to support the provider ecosystem without treating every institution as the same.

Brokers & Depository Participants

Investor education, account-readiness, structured introductions, onboarding coordination and status visibility.

Portfolio Managers

Strategy discovery, investor qualification, appointment, onboarding and multi-provider coordination.

Banks & Custodians

Banking, NRI/OCI, custody, settlement and account-structure journeys linked to investment access.

Fund Managers & AIFs

Fund education, investor interest, subscription workflows, documentation and servicing.

GIFT City / IFSC Providers

IFSC banking, funds, portfolio services, brokerage, custody and related foreign-currency access journeys.

Advisers, Wealth Managers & EAMs

Investor-supported journeys, provider coordination and controlled participation within the scope of their engagement.

Distributors & Intermediaries

Structured engagement and provider-linked workflows where the participant has a defined role in the journey.

️

Administrators & Ecosystem Participants

Role-based participation for registrars, administrators, trustees and other provider-approved participants.

Current market focus

Supporting India and GIFT City / IFSC Journeys

Facilon’s current Investor Access Navigator and Learning Centre focus on investment access to India and through GIFT City / IFSC. Service Provider workflows can be configured around the distinct requirements of each market.

India

Brokerage, demat, NRI/OCI banking, PMS, AIF, custody, advisory and other India-market access requirements.

GIFT City / IFSC

IFSC funds, portfolio services, brokerage, banking, custody and other permitted foreign-currency investment access structures.
