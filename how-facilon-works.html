The investor journey

From Understanding the Route to Ongoing Service

Each stage has a different purpose. Investors can begin with education and an Access Navigator before any formal provider engagement.

01

Learn

The Investor Learning Centre explains investor types, India and GIFT City routes, products, accounts, Service Provider roles, PAN, NRI/OCI banking, tax, compliance and investor-protection considerations.

Investor outcome

Understand the concepts before making a provider or onboarding decision.

02

Investor Access Navigator

The investor identifies who is investing, the relevant currency and what they want to do. Facilon derives the applicable market and shows the service, product/access requirement, Service Providers needed and indicative KYC/document readiness.

Investor outcome

A saved access requirement profile for India, GIFT City / IFSC, or both.

03

Discover & Appoint

Where the investor wants help finding remaining providers, Facilon can present relevant provider information and facilitate controlled engagement through Facilon Appoint.

Facilon Appoint

Structured discovery, provider profiles, information requests, meetings and controlled introductions.

04

Onboard & Track

Once a provider is selected, provider-specific data, documents, consents and reviews can be captured through Facilon Onboard while Facilon Status shows progress and pending actions.

Provider control

The Service Provider retains KYC, eligibility, compliance and final acceptance responsibility.

05

Instruct & Report

After onboarding, Facilon can support structured requests, instructions, acknowledgements, communications and approved reporting through Facilon Instruct and Facilon Report.

Ongoing service

The investor journey continues beyond account opening or product subscription.

Defined roles

Facilon Facilitates. Service Providers Retain Regulated Responsibility.

Facilon can

  • Educate investors about routes and provider roles
  • Structure investor access requirements
  • Support provider discovery and controlled introductions
  • Capture provider-configured data, documents and consents
  • Improve status visibility and pending-action tracking
  • Support structured instructions and reporting

The relevant Service Provider remains responsible for

  • Its regulated activity and licence obligations
  • KYC, AML and other compliance checks
  • Product eligibility and suitability where applicable
  • Final investor and account acceptance
  • Execution, custody or investment management where applicable
  • Provider-specific legal and contractual documentation
Important: Facilon does not provide investment, legal or tax advice, recommend a particular financial product, execute transactions or hold investor money or securities.
Current market scope

India and GIFT City / IFSC

The current Investor Access Navigator and Learning Centre cover investment access to India and through GIFT City / IFSC. The architecture can support additional markets as Facilon expands its market-specific content and provider network.
