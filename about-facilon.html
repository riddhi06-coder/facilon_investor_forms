About Facilon

Building a More Structured Investor Journey

Facilon was created to help investors and financial Service Providers navigate complex, multi-participant investment journeys through clearer education, structured workflows, controlled information sharing and better visibility.

Our Purpose

Facilon at a glance

A neutral digital facilitation and workflow platform supporting investors, authorised participants and Service Providers.

Investor education before formal engagement
Structured, consent-led digital journeys
Service Provider control over regulated decisions
Support across discovery, onboarding and servicing
Domestic and cross-border investor orientation
Why Facilon exists

Complex Journeys Need Better Coordination

Investment journeys often involve more than selecting a product or opening an account. They can require several providers, repeated information requests, route-specific documentation and ongoing communication.

The Problem We Are Addressing

Investors may not know which route applies to them, which Service Providers are required, which accounts or documents may be relevant or how long the process may take.

Service Providers may receive incomplete enquiries, repeat the same educational explanations, coordinate several participants through email and spreadsheets, and respond to frequent status requests.

Facilon is designed to bring structure to these interactions without replacing the regulated responsibilities, core systems or decision-making of the participating Service Provider.

Our Purpose

To help investors move from uncertainty to a clearer, better-prepared and more transparent engagement with relevant Service Providers.

Learn about the journey. Identify your access route. Prepare for the requirements. Connect when ready.
Our role

What Facilon Does—and Does Not Do

Clear role boundaries are central to Facilon’s operating model.

Facilon helps to

  • Educate investors about routes, provider roles and preparation requirements
  • Structure investor intent and access-requirement information
  • Enable controlled discovery and Service Provider engagement
  • Support provider-configured data, document and consent workflows
  • Improve status visibility and pending-action tracking
  • Support structured instructions, requests and reporting
  • Coordinate authorised participants through role-based access
  • Maintain evidence of consents, reviews, acknowledgements and status changes

Facilon does not

  • Provide investment advice or tax or legal advice
  • Recommend a particular investment product
  • Rank Service Providers based on expected returns
  • Guarantee account opening, eligibility or regulatory acceptance
  • Execute investment transactions
  • Hold investor money, securities or other assets
  • Replace the KYC, compliance or suitability responsibilities of a regulated entity
  • Make final investor-acceptance decisions on behalf of the Service Provider
Our operating principles

How We Approach the Investor Journey

Facilon’s platform and service model are guided by a consistent set of principles.

Clarity Before Action

Investors should understand their access route before entering a formal onboarding process.

Consent-Led Engagement

Information sharing should occur progressively, for defined purposes and with appropriate control.

Defined Accountability

Each participant retains responsibility for the regulated, contractual and operational role it performs.

Modular Adoption

Service Providers can begin with one use case and expand across the investor lifecycle over time.

Cross-Border Awareness

Investor residence, citizenship, tax status, market and route can materially affect the journey.

Transparency and Evidence

Investors and authorised participants should have clearer visibility into actions, observations and status.

Who we support

Designed for a Multi-Participant Ecosystem

One investment journey may involve several people and institutions. Facilon is designed to support these interactions within clearly defined roles.

Investors

Individuals, joint holders, families and legal entities navigating domestic or cross-border journeys.

Service Providers

Brokers, PMSs, custodians, banks, fund managers, distributors and other financial institutions.

Authorised Participants

Advisers, EAMs, family offices, representatives and provider-approved operational participants.

Ecosystem Participants

Trustees, registrars, administrators, brokers, banks, custodians and professional advisers involved in the journey.

The Facilon platform

Supporting the Journey From Education to Ongoing Service

Facilon’s modules are intended to work together as a facilitation layer across the investor lifecycle.

Stage 1

Learn and Assess

Investor education and Investor Access Navigator before formal provider engagement.

Stage 2

Discover and Engage

Structured provider information, requests, meetings and controlled introductions.

Stage 3

Onboard and Track

Provider-configured information, documents, consents, reviews and status visibility.

Stage 4

Instruct and Report

Ongoing service requests, instructions, communications, reports and investor servicing.

Trust framework

Built Around Privacy, Security and Operational Control

Facilon is intended to support clear information handling, participant accountability and evidence across the journey.

Purpose-Specific Processing

Information is collected and used for defined journey stages and purposes.

Progressive Disclosure

Detailed investor information need not be disclosed merely because an investor is exploring.

Role-Based Access

Users and authorised participants receive access based on assigned responsibilities.

Auditability

Consents, reviews, observations, acknowledgements and status changes can be evidenced.
