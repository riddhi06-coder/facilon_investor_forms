Investor-first clarity

Every Investment Journey Can Be Different

The relevant route can depend on who the investor is, where they reside, the market they wish to access, the service they require and their current readiness.

Investor Type

Individual, joint holder, family, legal entity or authorised representative.

Residence

Residence, citizenship, OCI status and tax residency may affect the access route.

Market

India domestic, GIFT City and other markets may involve distinct structures.

Service

Broking, PMS, custody, banking, fund and advisory journeys differ.

Provider Roles

Several regulated or authorised participants may be involved.

Readiness

Documents, accounts, KYC and route-specific prerequisites may need review.

Trust and accountability

Structured Around Control, Consent and Defined Roles

Facilon is intended to improve clarity and coordination while preserving the responsibilities of the investor and the relevant Service Provider.

Progressive Information Sharing

Investor information is collected and shared in stages for defined purposes.

Investor-Controlled Engagement

Provider introductions and detailed disclosures occur only after the required action and consent.

Provider-Led Decisions

The Service Provider retains responsibility for KYC, eligibility, acceptance and regulated activities.

Evidence and Auditability

Consents, reviews, observations, acknowledgements and status changes can be recorded.

Facilon is a facilitation and technology platform. It does not provide investment advice, recommend a particular Service Provider or investment product, execute investments, hold investor money or assets, or determine final investor eligibility.
